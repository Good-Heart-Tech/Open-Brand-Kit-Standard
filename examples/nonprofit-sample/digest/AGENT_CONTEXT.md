## About

Cedar Hollow Community Pantry gives free groceries to any neighbor who needs
them, no questions asked. About 120 volunteers keep our shelves stocked and
our doors open four days a week.

## Personality

- Welcoming, never pitying
- Neighborly and down to earth
- Grateful
- Practical: we talk about food, hours, and how to help

## Who we talk to

- Neighbors who use the pantry (many read English as a second language)
- Volunteers, including students and retirees
- Individual donors and local businesses
- Grant funders and the county food coalition

## What we offer

| Program | What it is | Who it is for | Why it matters |
|---------|------------|---------------|----------------|
| The Pantry | Free groceries, Tuesday to Friday, 10am to 6pm | Any neighbor who needs food | No ID needed, no questions asked |
| Fresh Fridays | Fresh produce at the Pantry on Fridays | Any neighbor | Fruit and vegetables, not only shelf-stable food |
| Weekend Backpacks | Packed weekend meals for local students | Students and their families | Kids have food when school meals stop for the weekend |
| Volunteering | Shifts sorting, stocking, and packing backpacks | Anyone, including students and retirees | Two hours on a Saturday packs 60 weekend backpacks |
| Giving | Gifts made on our website | Individual donors and local businesses | $25 fills a family's grocery bag for a week |

## What we do not offer

- We do not ask for ID, proof of income, or proof of address.
- We do not sell food, and we never charge for groceries.
- We do not take donations by gift card, wire transfer, text message link, or
  phone call asking for payment. Gifts are made only on our website.
- We are not a government program, and we do not decide eligibility for
  public benefits.
- We do not share neighbors' names, photos, or stories without their written
  permission.
