## About

Acme Docs is the Acme Labs knowledge base product. It helps growing
companies write down how things work, so knowledge does not walk out the
door when an employee moves on.

## Personality

Same as Acme Labs, plus:

- Organized and patient
- Encouraging to first-time writers

## Who we talk to

- Operations managers and team leads
- Executive directors handing off responsibilities

## What we offer

Acme Docs follows the Acme Labs offerings. Prices change often, so link to the
Acme Labs pricing page instead of quoting a number.

| Feature | What it is | Who it is for | Why choose it |
|---------|------------|---------------|---------------|
| Pages | One page for each process, policy, or how-to | Anyone writing down how things work | Start with one page and organize later |
| Spaces | A group of pages for a team or topic | Team leads and operations managers | Everyone knows where to look |
| Shared brand kit | Acme Docs reads the Acme Labs brand kit, so pages stay on brand | Marketing and operations leads | No extra setup for colors or wording |
| Free trial | Try Acme Docs before buying | Anyone evaluating Acme Docs | Your first page is three clicks away |

## What Acme Docs does not offer

- Writing or migrating documentation for customers. Acme Docs is software; we
  do not write your pages for you.
- An on-premises or self-hosted version.
- Features that are not released. Do not describe roadmap items as available.

## Voice and tone

Acme Docs uses the Acme Labs voice. In the product, lean encouraging: many
people writing documentation are doing it for the first time.

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|
| Empty states | Encouraging | "Start with one page. You can organize later." |
| Errors | Calm, specific | "We could not save this page. Check your connection and try again." |
| Onboarding emails | Warm, brief | "Your first page is three clicks away." |

## Always
